Help

How do I get started?

Create an account, then fill in your profile: your age, where you live, your education level and your skills. You can change it any time.

How does matching work?

An opportunity is shown to you only when all three of these are true:

  • Your age is inside the age range it asks for.
  • Your education level is one it accepts.
  • You have at least one of the skills it needs (or it needs none).

Your district does not stop an opportunity from showing. It only puts the ones near you first. Inside each group, the closest deadline comes first.

What does "Full" or "Partial" skill match mean?

Full means you have every skill listed. Partial means you have some of them.

Is every opportunity safe?

NextFit cannot check that listings are genuine. Always check an opportunity yourself and never pay a fee or give sensitive personal information first.

Reminders

Save an opportunity and you will see a reminder on your pages when its deadline is 14 days away or less.
